VoidTube is a free, open-source video streaming app for Android, designed around a clean interface, fewer interruptions, and greater control over how you watch.

Enjoy your favorite videos, music, and Shorts with a premium dark aesthetic, background playback, offline downloads, and a personalized viewing experience.

Features
Ad-free viewing — Enjoy videos without traditional advertisements, with SponsorBlock support for skipping sponsored segments.
Offline downloads — Download videos for viewing without an internet connection.
Background playback — Keep listening with the screen off or while using other apps.
Picture-in-picture — Watch videos in a floating window while multitasking.
Synced lyrics — Follow along with your favorite songs using synchronized lyrics.
Shorts — Enjoy a full-screen vertical video experience.
Personalization — Customize the theme, app icon, home screen, navigation, and more.
Enhanced discovery — Search filters, channel information, and video sorting.
Better video information — Dislike counts with Return YouTube Dislike and improved titles and thumbnails with DeArrow.
Flexible access — Sign in to access your subscriptions or use the app without an account.
Cast support — Enjoy supported content on your TV.
